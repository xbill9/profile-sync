Profile Drift- solving the problem you didn't know you had!

Every profile you own is a copy of your bio, and copies drift.

Every public profile you own says something slightly different about you. A step by step guide to treating your bio as source code, with one file, one tracker and an agent skill that checks the live sites.

What is in it:

- 🟢 9 of the 10 sites are Current, each confirmed on its live page
- 🟢 The certification count is computed from a list, so the headline and the About can no longer disagree
- 🟢 18 tests keep each failure beside a passing case
- ❌ 5 sites said 9 certifications the day after a tenth was issued, and 3 said 20+ years
